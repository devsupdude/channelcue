Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 27, 2026

ChannelCue helps you organize and search recent uploads from YouTube channels you already subscribe to. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information We Collect

When you sign in, ChannelCue may receive your app account identifier from Clerk. When you connect YouTube, ChannelCue requests read-only access to your YouTube account so it can load your subscribed channels and recent uploads.

ChannelCue stores your saved Google OAuth configuration, trial status, indexed channel metadata, and indexed video metadata so the app can search your subscriptions without repeatedly using expensive YouTube search requests.

Google User Data

ChannelCue uses Google user data only to provide the app features you request: loading your YouTube subscriptions, fetching recent uploads from those subscribed channels, and building a private searchable index for your account.

ChannelCue does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to third parties except as needed to provide and operate the service.

How We Store Data

Production app data is stored in Convex. Local development data may be stored in local JSON files. OAuth access tokens are used to communicate with Google APIs during your session.

Data Deletion

You can disconnect YouTube from inside ChannelCue. To request deletion of saved app data, email support@channelcue.com from the email address associated with your account.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@channelcue.com.
