Google OAuth Setup

For production use, add these values to your Google OAuth web client:

  • Authorized JavaScript origin: https://www.channelcue.com
  • Authorized redirect URI: https://www.channelcue.com/oauth2callback

Data Deletion

To request deletion of saved ChannelCue data, email support from the email address associated with your account.
